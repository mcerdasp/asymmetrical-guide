Change Management - ADKAR Approach 
OVERVIEW 
Successful change is How to facilitate change with one person. 
Awareness of the need for change 
A person's understanding the nature of the change, why is it being made, and the risk of not changing. Include info about the drivers that created that need for change, and "what's in it for me" 
Desire to support and participate in the change 
Willingness to support and engage. 
Knowledge of how to change 
Information, training and education necessary to know how to change. Info on behaviors, processes, tools, systems, skills, job roles and techniques. 
Ability to implement required skills and behaviors 
Turning knowledge into action. 
Reinforcement to sustain the change 
Internal and externa factors that sustain the change. Recognition, rewards, celebrations. 
Desire cannot come before Awareness - The awareness for the need to change stimulates our desire or resistance. 
Knowledge cannot come before Desire - We do not seek to know how to do something what we do not know. 
Ability cannot come before Knowledge - We cannot implement what we do not know. 
Reinforcement cannot come before Ability - We can only recognize and appreciate what has been achieved. 
AWARENESS 
Achieved when a person is aware and understand the nature of the change, why is it needed and the risks of not changing. 
Providing the "why" is a critical factor in managing change. At the first evidence of change, people begin to seek this information.
